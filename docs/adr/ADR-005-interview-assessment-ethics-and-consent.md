# ADR-005: Interview Assessment Ethics, Transparency & Consent

## Status
Accepted

## Context
Real-time conversation intelligence during high-stakes assessments (technical interviews, certification exams, academic assessments) poses significant ethical concerns regarding covert cheating, misrepresentation of candidate capabilities, and violation of consent laws regarding recording and transcription.

## Decision
1. **Anti-Covert-Cheating Policy**: UNICOM AI Interview Mode is explicitly engineered for **pre-session practice drills, transparent permitted accessibility assistance, and post-session analysis**. It does not provide covert or stealth overlays designed to bypass proctoring tools.
2. **Consent Notifications**: Prominent in-app notifications and generated reports explicitly record that transcripts and assessments are authored with user consent in compliance with local recording laws.
3. **Structured Coaching vs. Teleprompting**: The interview evaluator provides rubric-based scoring (clarity, depth, structure, delivery, correctness), identify strengths/weaknesses, and generates study plans rather than generating real-time deceptive answers.

## Consequences
- **Positive**: Ethical compliance; prevents misuse; empowers candidate learning and skill development.
- **Negative**: Users seeking surreptitious assistance must look elsewhere, as UNICOM explicitly rejects covert cheating features.
